# CyberRakshak India Safety & Escalation Guide

CyberRakshak is an education, prevention, triage and guidance layer. It does not replace law-enforcement, banks, telecom operators or official cybercrime systems.

## Financial cyber fraud

1. Contact your bank/payment provider immediately using its official support channel.
2. For urgent financial cyber-fraud reporting in India, use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and the 1930 cyber-fraud helpline.
3. Preserve transaction IDs, dates, amounts, screenshots and relevant messages without forwarding suspicious content unnecessarily.

## Cybercrime reporting

Use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal for cybercrime complaints and its suspect-reporting/checking services.

## Telecom and SIM-related fraud

Use Sanchar Saathi for supported telecom-safety services such as reporting suspected fraud communications, checking mobile connections issued in your name, and reporting lost/stolen handsets.

## General security advisories

Use CERT-In advisories and cyber-safety guidance for current defensive recommendations.

## CyberRakshak product rule

The application should always distinguish:

- **What CyberRakshak detected** — a local, explainable assessment.
- **What the user should do now** — practical defensive steps.
- **Where to report** — official channels.
- **What CyberRakshak cannot do** — investigate, freeze funds, recover accounts, or guarantee that a message/site is safe.

## Privacy rule

Do not request passwords, OTPs, card PINs, CVV values, authentication tokens, recovery codes or other secrets as part of safety analysis. User-submitted scam material should be minimized, protected and retained only when there is a documented product need.
