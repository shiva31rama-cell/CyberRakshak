# Accessibility Acceptance Checklist

CyberRakshak should remain usable for people with different devices, literacy levels and assistive technologies.

- [ ] Every interactive control has an accessible name.
- [ ] Keyboard focus is visible and follows a logical order.
- [ ] Color is never the only signal for risk severity.
- [ ] Risk states include text labels such as Low, Medium or High.
- [ ] Form errors are associated with their inputs and announced where appropriate.
- [ ] Touch targets are large enough for mobile use.
- [ ] Layout remains usable at narrow mobile widths.
- [ ] Text has sufficient contrast.
- [ ] Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- [ ] Loading and success/error states are understandable without animation.
- [ ] Language selection is persistent and does not destroy entered form data.
- [ ] Official emergency/reporting actions are clearly distinguishable from ordinary navigation.
- [ ] Important safety instructions use plain language before technical terminology.
